AKVS MAHAL

AKVS Thirumana Mandapam is a wedding and event venue located in Arani, Tamil Nadu. It's situated on Malli Pillaiyar Koil Street in the Kosapalayam area, with the postal code 632301. This hall is commonly used for weddings, receptions, and other social gatherings.

City Reviews

Facilities and Features:

Main Hall: Suitable for weddings, receptions, and other social gatherings.

Dining Area: Available for guests, though some reviews suggest it may need better maintenance.

Guest Rooms: Rooms are available for the bride, groom, and guests, but accessibility may be limited as there is no lift facility.

Parking: Limited parking space, which has been noted as a concern by some visitors.
