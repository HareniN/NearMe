VEDAPURI ESHWARAR TEMPLE - ARNI

A Peaceful and Ancient Shiva Temple

Vedapuri Eswarar Temple is a historic and well-known Shiva temple located in Arni, a town in the Tiruvannamalai district of Tamil Nadu, India. The presiding deity of this temple is Lord Shiva, worshipped here as Vedapureeswarar, and Goddess Parvati as Balambigai.

The temple has beautiful architecture with traditional South Indian style gopuram (tower), spacious prakarams (corridors), and a serene atmosphere that offers peace to all devotees. It is one of the ancient temples in Arni and holds great religious significance.

Special Features:

Deity: Lord Shiva (Vedapuri eswarar) and Goddess Balambigai

Location: Arni town, Tiruvannamalai District, Tamil Nadu

Architecture: Traditional Dravidian style

Festivals: Maha Shivaratri, Pradosham, and other important Shiva-related festivals

Many people from nearby villages and towns visit the temple regularly to seek blessings. It is also a peaceful place for meditation and prayer.
